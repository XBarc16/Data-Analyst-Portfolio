# Crime Statistics and Trends Analysis Dashboard

## The dashboard is designed to provide the Datara Police with insights into crime patterns, helping them identify high-risk areas and times, as well as understand the types of crimes prevalent in different regions. This information can aid in resource allocation, strategic planning, and targeted interventions to reduce crime rates.

## Summary of the Power BI Crime Analysis Dashboard:

### Overview Metrics:

Total Crimes: 38.44K incidents reported.
Crime Rate per 100k: 15.38K, indicating the rate of crime per 100,000 residents.
Most Impacted ZIP: 85263, highlighting the area with the highest crime concentration.

### Trend Analysis:

A line graph showing crime incidence from 2010 to 2023, illustrating trends over time with a noticeable dip around 2020.

### Crime Distribution by Category:

A bar chart displaying the distribution of crimes by category, with Larceny-Theft being the most prevalent, followed by Aggravated Assault and Motor Vehicle Theft.

### Crime by Day/Night:

A donut chart comparing crime occurrences during the day versus night, showing a higher incidence during the day.

### Seasonal Crime Analysis:

A donut chart breaking down crime occurrences by season, with winter having the highest crime rate.

### Detailed Crime Data by ZIP Code:

A matrix presenting the number of crimes across different categories for various ZIP codes, allowing for a granular analysis of crime distribution.
